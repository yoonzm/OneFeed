What stays in your hands

Not another content platform. A personal interface between you and the platforms.

Focus Paper reading

Give titles, previews, authors, media, and interactions one clear hierarchy, with light and dark appearances for comfortable reading.

Local display filters

Hide read items and recommendations, or filter by keyword, author, platform, and content type. Matching items are never removed from the source.

Preferences that follow you

Website order, image display, appearance, recent platforms, and filter rules stay in Chrome's local extension storage.

The original site is always there

Likes, comments, and saves are delegated to the source when reliable. Pause OneFeed at any time to restore the original page immediately.

Local-first privacy

Your reading does not need to pass through our server first.

The current version does not send page content, browsing history, or extension settings to a developer server or third party. Parsing, sanitization, filtering, and rendering all happen in your browser.

Questions, answered

The important questions, answered plainly.

Does OneFeed upload my browsing data?

No. In the current version, page parsing, content sanitization, display filters, and rendering all happen locally in your browser. Page content, browsing history, and extension settings are not sent to a developer server or third party.

Is OneFeed an RSS reader?

No. OneFeed works on supported websites you already have open. It keeps the original content, signed-in state, and essential interactions while rearranging supported feeds and detail pages into one consistent interface.

Can I still use the original website?

Yes. Supported interactions are delegated to the original page. Complex features that cannot be delegated reliably stay on the original site, and you can pause OneFeed at any time to restore the page immediately.

Which websites are supported?

OneFeed currently supports selected feeds and detail pages on Weibo, X, Xiaohongshu, Reddit, Zhihu, Hacker News, Linux DO, V2EX, and 36Kr. See the project README for exact route coverage.
